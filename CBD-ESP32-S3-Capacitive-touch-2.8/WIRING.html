ESP32-S3 multi-radio wiring

A solder-ready interconnect for a portable ESP32-S3 handheld: a CC1101 sub-GHz transceiver and an nRF24L01+ (PA/LNA) sharing one SPI bus, an ATGM336H GPS on UART, and a TP4056 LiPo charging path — all on a single S3 that already carries a built-in display.

MCU ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 Sub-GHz CC1101 · SPI 2.4 GHz nRF24L01+PA+LNA · SPI GNSS ATGM336H · UART 9600 Power TP4056 + 1S LiPo
Checked

Pins were cross-verified against ESP32-S3 silicon constraints (strapping / flash / octal-PSRAM / native-USB), per-module voltage & logic levels, and the TP4056 power path. The radio SPI bus was deliberately moved off GPIO 9–14 — the S3's default LCD cluster — so it won't fight your built-in display. Before soldering, still confirm your specific board's display/touch/SD pins (see gotcha ①).

§4Power path

Boost the cell to a stable 5 V so the board's onboard 3.3 V regulator stays above its dropout across the whole discharge curve — then feed the hungry nRF24 from its own regulator.

USB 5V

Charge input

5 V into TP4056 IN+/IN− only. This is the charger's port — not the board's.

→
TP4056

Protected charger

DW01A + FS8205A variant (has OUT+/OUT−). Set Rprog to ~0.5–1C of the cell.

→
1S LiPo

Protected cell

On BAT+/BAT−. 3.0–4.2 V. Use a cell with its own PCM.

→
Boost 5V

Step-up ≥1 A

From OUT+/OUT− to a stable 5.0 V. Add 100–220µF bulk on the rail.

→
ESP32-S3

VIN / 5V pin

Onboard AMS1117 makes the display's 3.3 V. nRF24 gets a separate 3.3 V LDO off this 5 V.

  • FATALOne power entry at a time. The board's own USB 5 V and the boost 5 V must never be live together — that back-feed / two-charger path is what smokes things. Flash with the battery/boost disconnected.
  • CARETP4056 is not a power-path charger. Running the load while charging corrupts its charge-termination sensing. Charge with the device off, or swap in an MCP73871 / BQ24074 for true use-while-charging.
  • CAREDon't hang the nRF24 off the display's 3.3 V. Its TX current spikes will sag the shared rail and glitch the LCD. Give it a dedicated LDO + local bulk cap.

§5Before you solder

The failures that actually happen on this exact build, ranked by how much they hurt.

① Fatal · do first

Audit your display's pins

Dump your board's panel init and record its SCK/MOSI/MISO/CS/DC/RST/BL + touch + SD GPIOs. Real S3-CYD boards put the TFT on GPIO10–13 with backlight on 21/27/45. This map dodges that cluster — confirm your board doesn't also use 5/6/7/8/17/18/38/39/40.

② Fatal · silicon

GPIO 22–25 don't exist on S3

Those numbers are classic-ESP32 only. The WontHound repo's GPIO23 radio comments are legacy from the ESP32 CYD — never assign them on an S3.

③ Fatal · voltage

Never 5 V on the radios

nRF24 abs-max ≈3.6 V, CC1101 ≈3.9 V, and neither has 5 V-tolerant IO. VCC and every logic pin stay at 3.3 V. 5 V = dead module.

④ Important

nRF24 needs a stiff 3.3 V

Dedicated LDO + 47µF & 100nF right at the header. This is the #1 cause of the classic "transmits nothing / resets on TX" failure.

⑤ Important

10k pull-ups on both CS lines

GPIO2 (CC1101) and GPIO38 (nRF24) each need a 10k → 3.3 V so neither radio drives MISO during the boot float window.

⑥ Important

Battery sense stays on ADC1

GPIO4 = ADC1_CH3. ADC2 is unusable while Wi-Fi is on — never move the divider to a GPIO11–20 pin.

⑦ Important

Power GPS at 3.3 V

Then its UART TX can't over-drive GPIO18. Keep the crossover (GPS-TX→ESP-RX, ESP-TX→GPS-RX) and start at 9600 8N1.

⑧ Important

Use the protected TP4056

The one with OUT+/OUT− pads, plus a protected cell. A bare red TP4056 has no protection and will deep-discharge the LiPo.

⑨ Tip

Size Rprog to the cell

Default 1.2k ≈ 1 A cooks a small pack. Iprog ≈ 1200 / Rprog A — e.g. 2k–2.4k for a 500 mAh cell.

⑩ Tip

Keep these pins clear

Native-USB GPIO19/20, strapping 0/3/45/46, flash 26–32, octal-PSRAM 33–37, UART0 console 43/44, and the common RGB-LED GPIO48.

§6Extra parts checklist

Beyond the four modules and the board — the passives and power parts that make it actually work.

1×47 µF electrolytic/tantalum + 100 nF ceramic — at nRF24 header
1×10 µF + 100 nF — at CC1101 VCC
1×100 nF — at GPS VCC
2×10 kΩ — CS pull-ups (GPIO2, GPIO38)
2×100 kΩ (or 220 kΩ) + 100 nF — battery divider
1×Protected TP4056 module (OUT+/OUT− pads)
1×Protected 1S LiPo — capacity to suit Rprog
1×Boost converter → 5.0 V, ≥1 A (e.g. MT3608-class, set output)
1×3.3 V LDO for nRF24 (low-dropout, off the 5 V rail)
1×100–220 µF bulk cap — on the 5 V boost rail
Reference, not gospel. Board variants differ — always verify against your specific ESP32-S3 board's own pinout and your modules' datasheets before powering up. Double-check every 3.3 V rail with a meter before the first connect, and bring the battery up last.
